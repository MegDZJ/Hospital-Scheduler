    About Project

    Hospital Priority Queue & Bed Allocation Scheduler

    An Operating Systems project that models a hospital as a scheduler: patients are processes, beds are the limited resource, and a priority queue with FCFS tie-breaking decides who is served next. All scheduling runs in C; this page only displays the state.

    • Priority: severity + age adjustment, highest first.
    • Tie-break: earlier arrival, then earlier registration (FCFS).
    • Non-preemptive: an occupied bed is never taken away for a higher-priority arrival.
    • Clock: simulated minutes; it only moves when you advance it or the simulation is Running.
    Academic simulation only. The priority rules, severity scores and occupancy durations are illustrative demonstration parameters. They are not medical triage standards and must not be used for any actual clinical decision.
